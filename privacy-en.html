Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

In short: DZ Video Upscale processes video locally on your Mac. It never uploads any video files and does not collect your personal data.

1. What we process

DZ Video Upscale is a locally running video upscaling tool. The video files you choose are only read into your Mac's memory and disk for processing, and you save the results to a location you choose.

We do not:

  • Upload, copy, or back up your video files to any server
  • Collect video content, frames, audio, or any information within them
  • Collect your name, contact details, location, or device identifiers for analytics
  • Use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs

2. Network connections

Upscaling and frame interpolation run entirely on your device and do not require an internet connection. Besides App Store system calls, the app may make network time-sync requests when an accurate time is needed, so that its time-related features run based on the correct current time.

These requests contain no video content, device identifiers, account information, or any personal data, and we do not collect or report information through them. The result is used only on your device and is not linked to any identity. If time sync fails (for example, while offline), core features such as video processing are unaffected and the app remains fully usable; the relevant time-related features will be calibrated again once sync is restored.

In addition, the App Store system performs network communication to verify and process subscription purchases. This data is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy, and we cannot access your payment information.

3. Data stored on your device

The app may store a small number of usage-related preferences on your Mac (for example, the last selected export folder or interface options). This data stays on your Mac, never leaves the device, and is removed when you delete the app.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are completed through App Store in-app purchases. We never access, store, or process your credit card number, billing address, or other payment information. Subscription status is verified by Apple's system. Refund requests are submitted through Apple and handled under its refund policy.

5. Children's privacy

This app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal data, there is no data collection involving children.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third party in any form — because we do not collect data in the first place.

7. Your rights

Because the app collects no personal data, there are generally no personal information records to access, correct, or delete. If you believe we hold personal data related to this app, you can contact us at the email below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially (for example, if a future feature requires internet access), we will update this page and change the effective date. We recommend reviewing this page before using the app.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, email us at china.jsy@qq.com.
