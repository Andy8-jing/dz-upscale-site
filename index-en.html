Upscale your footage to 4K, right on your Mac

What it solves

It genuinely runs locally

All inference happens on your Mac. No account system, no cloud queue, no handing your footage to a third party.

Matched by aspect ratio, not stretched

16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 4:5 and 3:2 each get their own inference shape. Landscape footage never gets squashed square and stretched back.

Uses the hardware you paid for

The Neural Engine handles upscaling, the GPU handles encoding. While a task runs, the parts of the machine that should be busy are busy.

Frame interpolation, if you want it

Besides raising resolution, you can raise the frame rate at the same time so motion reads smoother.

Questions people ask

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed on your Mac, and the app never reads or sends video content anywhere. Only purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store system.

What hardware do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later) and macOS 14.0 or later. Intel Macs don't get Neural Engine acceleration.

Which formats are supported?

Common formats such as H.264 and HEVC are read directly. The output is a standard macOS-compatible video file you can drop straight into an editor.

Are there limits?

The free version limits output resolution and the length of a single run. Subscribing removes those limits; the exact current specs are shown in the app.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and can be reviewed and managed at any time in System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your Mac. Refunds follow Apple's policy.
